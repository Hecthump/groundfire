Groundfire: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Groundfire ("the game") handles, and why. The game is published by Thump Labs ("we", "us").

The short version

  • The game has no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your game progress is stored on your own device. We do not receive it.
  • The game shows optional advertisements, supplied by Google. Google collects some information to deliver and measure them.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (upgrades, currencies, records and settings) is saved on your device. It stays there unless you choose to copy a "save code" from the game's settings in order to move your progress to another device. We have no servers that receive this information, and we cannot see it.

If device backup is switched on for your Google account, Android may include the game's data in your own backup. That is controlled by you in your device settings.

Advertising

The game only shows an advertisement when you tap a button that offers a reward for watching one. No advertisement appears by itself.

Advertisements are provided by Google AdMob. To deliver, limit and measure them, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address, which indicates your approximate location;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version and language;
  • information about your interaction with advertisements, and diagnostic data.

Google acts as an independent controller of this information. Its use is described in Google's Privacy Policy and at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices. Where the law requires it (including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before personalised advertising is used, and you can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings, then Ad choices. On any device you can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised advertising, in your device's Google settings under Ads.

Purchases

If you buy something in the game, the payment is processed by Google Play. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made, so that the game can give you what you bought, but we never receive your card number or other payment details. Purchases are linked to your Google account by Google. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you allow it, the game can show reminders on your device, for example when a timed upgrade has finished. These are created on your device. They are not sent from a server, and you can switch them off in the game's settings or in your device settings.

Information we collect ourselves

None. We do not operate analytics, user accounts or servers for the game, and we do not sell or share personal information.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Keeping and deleting your information

Because your progress exists only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the game's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the game. For information held by Google, use the controls in your Google account.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal information. As we hold none ourselves, requests about advertising or purchase information should be made to Google. You are also welcome to contact us with any question.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
